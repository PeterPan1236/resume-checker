See the resume the way a screener sees it.

Every submission runs two passes: a deterministic rule engine that reproduces what an applicant tracking system can and cannot read, and a hiring-manager review of the actual content. The result is a written assessment: scorecard, requirement-by-requirement compliance, and a prioritized remediation list.
