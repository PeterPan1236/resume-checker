Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform
Northstar Commerce · San Francisco, CA (hybrid, 3 days on site)

About the team
The Payments Platform team owns authorization, settlement, ledgering and payout for every
transaction on Northstar. We process roughly 40M transactions a month across nine markets.

Minimum qualifications
- 7+ years of professional backend engineering experience, at least 3 of them on payments,
  billing, ledger or another financially consequential system.
- Production experience with Go or Java. We are a Go shop; strong Java engineers who want to
  switch are welcome.
- Demonstrated ownership of a service in production: on-call, incident response, postmortems.
- Experience designing and evolving relational schemas at scale (PostgreSQL preferred),
  including online migrations against live traffic.
- Practical experience with idempotency, exactly-once semantics and reconciliation of
  distributed financial state.
- Experience integrating third-party payment providers such as Stripe, Adyen or Braintree.
- Track record of measurable reliability or latency improvements, with numbers you can defend.
- Strong written communication: design docs, RFCs, incident writeups.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with Kubernetes and Terraform in a production environment.
- Exposure to PCI DSS scope reduction or SOX controls.
- Experience mentoring engineers or leading a small team.
- Familiarity with event-driven architectures using Kafka.
- Multi-currency or cross-border payments experience.

What you will do
- Design and ship services that move money correctly under partial failure.
- Own the reconciliation pipeline end to end, from ingestion through ledger close.
- Partner with Risk, Treasury and Compliance on controls and audit evidence.
- Raise the reliability bar: SLOs, error budgets, load testing before peak season.

Benefits
Competitive salary, equity, 401k match, comprehensive medical and dental, generous parental
leave, annual learning budget. Northstar Commerce is an equal opportunity employer.
